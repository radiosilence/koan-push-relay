- **Audit fixes.** Ended Live Activities and gone devices the relay could not record are forgotten once their handles have expired, rather than kept until the in-memory set fills and then let through to Apple; `koan_relay_gone` and `koan_relay_ended_activities` report their sizes. A push answered as gone no longer holds a place among pushes waiting on Apple while the revocation is written. Behind a proxy, a forwarded-address line that is not text is no longer passed over when counting back to the caller, so a caller cannot shift the count onto an address it wrote. An SQLite error reading the revocation table at startup stops the relay rather than being read as zero. `koan-relay-client` keeps a base URL's last path segment when it has no trailing slash: `https://example.org/relay` now reaches `/relay/v1/…`, where it used to reach `/v1/…` at the host's root.
